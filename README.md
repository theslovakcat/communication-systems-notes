## Contents

### Part 1: Communication Fundamentals and Signals Review

- Communication systems, channels, and modulation
- Analog and digital signals; source and channel coding
- Channel impairments: attenuation, distortion, interference, and noise
- Basic signals, sampling, energy, and power
- Fourier series and Fourier transform properties
- Convolution, duality, and sampling
- Parseval's and Rayleigh's theorems

### Part 2: Spectral Density and Correlation

- Energy and power spectral density
- Energy and power of signal sums
- Time averages, inner products, and the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality
- Autocorrelation and cross-correlation
- Correlation of energy and power signals
- Spectral density and autocorrelation; Wiener–Khinchin theorem exercise
- Input–output correlation in linear time-invariant systems

### Part 3: Filters and the Hilbert Transform

- Filter types and system interconnections
- Practical filter responses and bandwidth definitions
- Equivalent noise bandwidth
- Butterworth, Chebyshev, inverse Chebyshev, Bessel, and elliptic filters
- Hilbert filter frequency and impulse responses
- Properties and examples of the Hilbert transform
- Inverse Hilbert transform and the DC component
